# OSS and commercial boundary

The commercial hypothesis is that teams will pay to **operate trustworthy cross-platform decisions continuously**, while the measurement protocol should stay inspectable and portable. No revenue, partnership, or platform acquisition is assumed.

| Inspectable baseline, shipped or planned | Commercial service candidate |
| --- | --- |
| Versioned CSV/plan contracts, strict reconciliation, economics, estimator, verifier | Managed, permissioned source ingestion and reliability operations |
| Synthetic fixtures, negative tests, CI and evaluation methodology | Enterprise tenant isolation, retention controls, SLA and support |
| Read-only provider export adapters once validated | Connector maintenance and managed deployment at scale |
| Portable scorecard and BI snapshot formats | Continuous monitoring, approved experiment operations and review workflow |
| Reference agent proposal/evidence schema | Customer-specific models and operational expertise |
| Public benchmark methodology | Opt-in, aggregated benchmark network with explicit data rights |

The possible Vercel feature is a report of **contribution profit per accepted conversion** for a Flags experiment. The possible Cloudflare feature is an outcome-join and sampling-aware analysis package for edge telemetry. Build each as an adapter to the open protocol so a buyer or partner can inspect the math. A Marketplace listing or OEM license is a distribution route, not evidence that either company wants to buy it.

For an OEM negotiation, distinguish a nonexclusive integration license, a support agreement, and any exclusive field-of-use agreement. Define source ownership, derivative rights, data access, attribution, maintenance obligations, termination and portability before granting exclusivity. Have counsel review binding terms. The cross-platform protocol, customer relationship and independently developed managed service should remain viable under the proposed contract.

## Unit economics to measure in each pilot

```text
monthly gross margin = contracted recurring revenue
                     - ingestion and storage cost
                     - evaluation compute
                     - model inference
                     - support and incident labor

customer ROI = verified incremental contribution profit
             - subscription and implementation fees
             - customer operating and experiment costs
```

Report the denominator of every unit metric: per accepted conversion, per million ingested events, per verified decision, per experiment, and per tenant-month. Price a pilot against measured workload and support obligations; this repository does not contain a defensible market price or valuation. The first commercial gate is two or more independent, consented pilots with repeatable reports and a buyer willing to renew.
